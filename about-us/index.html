About Us

We train small business people, chamas, SHGs, and local interest groups on how to use greener energy and financial literacy. The skills that come from the training help to cut costs, shield their income from climatic interruptions, and instill financial discipline.

Our trainees can be referred to our partner lending institutions where they are offered the opportunity to get green loans at concessionary rates of 8% - 11% annually. This enables us to focus on doing what we know best, namely providing quality training, while offering the graduates access to loans without difficulty.
