Syllabus

What you learn across two weeks.

The training follows a two week structure. Week one covers greener energy and climate response. Week two covers financial literacy and credit readiness. While the financial literacy component is consistent across all cohorts, the rest of the training is fluid and tailored to each group or business. We adapt the content, examples, case studies, and assignments to match your actual activity.

Week One: Greener Energy & Climate Response

You learn how to reduce your energy costs by shifting from diesel, petrol, kerosene, and charcoal to cleaner alternatives available in Kenya: solar systems, improved cookstoves, biogas, and e mobility. You also learn to protect your income from climate shocks like floods, heatwaves, and water shortages.

Week Two: Financial Literacy & Credit Readiness

This week covers the basics of bookkeeping, understanding loan terms, and managing borrowed money effectively. All participants learn how to track income and expenses, separate business from personal funds, and plan for repayment.

How tailoring works

Before training begins, we conduct an assessment call. We ask about your sector, your size, your current challenges, and your goals. We then adapt the curriculum to fit. You do not sit through content that does not apply to you. You learn exactly what you need to cut costs, protect your income, and manage your finances.

What you receive after training

  • A verifiable certificate
  • Eligibility for green loans at 8–11% through our partner lenders
  • Access to alumni resources and updates
